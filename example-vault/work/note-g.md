# Note G

Note G is the final note in Ada Lovelace's series A–G accompanying her English translation of Luigi Menabrea's account of the Analytical Engine. It contains a table of operations for calculating Bernoulli numbers with the proposed machine.[^note-g-overview]

It is part of the [Menabrea Translation and Notes A–G](menabrea-translation-and-notes.md "{type: part_of}").

The note is traditionally attributed to [Ada Lovelace](../people/ada-lovelace.md "{type: authored_by, year: 1843, attribution: \"traditional\"}").

Lovelace developed the procedure in exchange with [Charles Babbage](../people/charles-babbage.md "{type: developed_with, year: 1843}"), who later said that he supplied the mathematical formulas while she converted them into a step-by-step table and detected an error.[^note-g-overview]

The procedure was designed for the [Analytical Engine](analytical-engine.md "{type: designed_for}").

Note G is often described as the first published computer program. The careful qualifier is “published”: Babbage had prepared earlier programs that were not published, and the Engine was not completed in their lifetimes.[^ada-program]
