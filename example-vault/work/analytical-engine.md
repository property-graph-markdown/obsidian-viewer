# Analytical Engine

The Analytical Engine was Charles Babbage's design for a digital, mechanical general-purpose computer. It was described from 1837 but was not completed during Babbage's lifetime.[^engine-overview]

## Why it matters in computing history

The design included an arithmetic unit, memory, and programmable control flow with conditional branching and loops. It is therefore described in modern terms as a programmable general-purpose computer.[^engine-programming]

## Ada Lovelace's contribution

Ada explained the machine's logical operation in her [translation and Notes A–G](menabrea-translation-and-notes.md "{type: documented_by, from: 1842, to: 1843, language: \"English\"}"). Most importantly, she argued that such a machine could manipulate rule-bound symbols, not only numerical quantities.[^engine-overview]
