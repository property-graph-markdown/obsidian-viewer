# Ada Programming Language

Ada is a structured, statically typed programming language developed for the United States Department of Defense and first released in 1980.[^ada-language-overview]

In 1979, the “Green” proposal designed by Jean Ichbiah's team was selected and named Ada in honor of Ada Lovelace. The reference manual was approved on 10 December 1980, her birthday, and the MIL-STD-1815 designation refers to her birth year.[^ada-language-history]

The language is a later namesake, not a product of Lovelace's own work. The graph's `NAMESAKE_OF` relationship therefore represents commemoration, not authorship.
