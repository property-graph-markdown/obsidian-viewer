# Charles Babbage

Charles Babbage (1791–1871) was an English mathematician, inventor, and designer of early mechanical calculating machines.[^babbage-overview]

Ada met him through Mary Somerville in 1833. The meeting developed into a long friendship and working relationship centered on Babbage's calculating machines.[^ada-overview]

Babbage designed the [Analytical Engine](../work/analytical-engine.md "{type: designed, from: 1837}") from 1837.[^babbage-overview]

His 1840 lecture in Turin was transcribed by Luigi Menabrea. That text later became the basis for Ada's English translation and Notes.[^ada-notes] [Turin](../places/turin.md "{type: lectured_in, year: 1840, topic: \"Analytical Engine\"}")
