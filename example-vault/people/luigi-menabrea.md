# Luigi Menabrea

Luigi Federico Menabrea (1809–1896) was an Italian engineer, mathematician, officer, and later politician.[^menabrea-overview]

After Babbage's Turin lecture in 1840, Menabrea wrote a French description of the Analytical Engine that appeared in 1842.[^ada-notes] [Analytical Engine](../work/analytical-engine.md "{type: documented, year: 1842, language: \"French\"}")

Ada translated that text into English in 1842–1843 and added Notes A–G. This was primarily a relationship between works and publications; the cited Wikipedia sections do not establish a close personal collaboration.[^ada-notes]
