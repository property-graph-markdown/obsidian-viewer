# Anne Blunt, 15th Baroness Wentworth

Anne Isabella Noel Blunt (22 September 1837 – 15 December 1917), known as Lady Anne Blunt, was a British author, musician, artist, and Arabian horse breeder. She was the daughter of Ada Lovelace and William King-Noel.[^anne-blunt-overview]

She was a child of [Ada Lovelace](ada-lovelace.md "{type: child_of}").

She was a child of [William King-Noel](william-king-noel.md "{type: child_of}").

Her birth is represented by [1837 — Anne Isabella Is Born](../events/1837-anne-isabella-born.md "{type: participated_in, role: \"subject\"}").

With her husband Wilfrid Blunt, she co-founded the Crabbet Arabian Stud and the Sheykh Obeyd estate near Cairo. Shortly before her death, she inherited the title of 15th Baroness Wentworth.[^anne-blunt-overview]
