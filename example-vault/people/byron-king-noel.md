# Byron King-Noel, Viscount Ockham

Byron King-Noel (12 May 1836 – 1 September 1862), styled Viscount Ockham, was a British peer and naval officer. He was the eldest son of Ada Lovelace and William King-Noel.[^byron-king-noel-overview]

He was a child of [Ada Lovelace](ada-lovelace.md "{type: child_of}").

He was a child of [William King-Noel](william-king-noel.md "{type: child_of}").

His birth is represented by [1836 — Byron King-Noel Is Born](../events/1836-byron-king-noel-born.md "{type: participated_in, role: \"subject\"}").

After his maternal grandmother died in 1860, he became the 12th Baron Wentworth. He died unmarried and without children in 1862.[^byron-king-noel-overview]
