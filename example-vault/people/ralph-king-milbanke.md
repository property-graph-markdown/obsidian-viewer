# Ralph King-Milbanke, 2nd Earl of Lovelace

Ralph Gordon King-Milbanke (2 July 1839 – 28 August 1906), 2nd Earl of Lovelace, was a British nobleman and author. He was the youngest child of Ada Lovelace and William King-Noel.[^ralph-king-milbanke-overview]

He was a child of [Ada Lovelace](ada-lovelace.md "{type: child_of}").

He was a child of [William King-Noel](william-king-noel.md "{type: child_of}").

His birth is represented by [1839 — Ralph Gordon Is Born](../events/1839-ralph-gordon-born.md "{type: participated_in, role: \"subject\"}").

He succeeded his elder brother as Baron Wentworth in 1862 and his father as Earl of Lovelace in 1893. His 1906 book *Astarte* addressed the history and reputation of his maternal grandfather, Lord Byron.[^ralph-king-milbanke-overview]
