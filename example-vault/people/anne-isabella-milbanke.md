# Anne Isabella Milbanke

Anne Isabella Noel Byron (1792–1860), 11th Baroness Wentworth and usually known as Lady Byron, was a reformer and Ada Lovelace's mother.[^lady-byron]

After separating from Lord Byron, Ada's upbringing remained within the influence of her mother and maternal family. Lady Byron's concern that Ada might inherit traits from her father was one reason for Ada's early mathematical education.[^ada-childhood]
