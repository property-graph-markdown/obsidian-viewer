# Andrew Crosse

Andrew Crosse (1784–1855) was an English electrical experimenter.[^crosse-overview]

Ada visited Crosse in 1844 to learn how to conduct electrical experiments while exploring a possible mathematical model of the nervous system.[^ada-work]

His role is represented as a specific research contact rather than as a co-author of Ada's computing work.
