# Augustus De Morgan

Augustus De Morgan (1806–1871) was a British mathematician and logician.[^de-morgan-overview]

During the 1840s, he supported Ada's advanced mathematical studies. These included calculus and Bernoulli numbers, which became relevant to her well-known procedure for the Analytical Engine.[^ada-education]

His role is therefore modeled precisely as teacher and mathematical supporter, not as co-author of Ada's publication.
