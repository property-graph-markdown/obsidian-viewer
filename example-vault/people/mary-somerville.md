# Mary Somerville

Mary Somerville (1780–1872) was a Scottish mathematician, scientist, and science writer.[^somerville-overview]

She was one of Ada's private teachers. Ada and Somerville became friends and corresponded for years.[^ada-education] [^ada-adult-years]

Somerville introduced Ada to Charles Babbage in 1833. Her roles in this bundle are education, sustained contact, and introduction; she is not labeled as a co-author of the Notes.[^ada-adult-years]
