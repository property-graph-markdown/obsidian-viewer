# William King-Noel

William King-Noel (1805–1893), 1st Earl of Lovelace, was an English nobleman and scientist.[^william-overview]

He married Ada Byron in 1835. He became Viscount Ockham and Earl of Lovelace in 1838, making Ada Countess of Lovelace. The couple had three children.[^william-first-marriage]

Their children were [Byron King-Noel](byron-king-noel.md "{type: \"parent_of\", birth_order: 1}"), [Anne Blunt](anne-blunt.md "{type: parent_of, birth_order: 2}"), and [Ralph King-Milbanke](ralph-king-milbanke.md "{type: parent_of, birth_order: 3}").

[Horsley Towers](../places/horsley-towers.md "{type: resided_at, from: 1845}") became the family's principal home in 1845.
