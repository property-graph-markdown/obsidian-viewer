# Lord Byron

George Gordon Byron, 6th Baron Byron (1788–1824), was an English poet and Ada Lovelace's father.[^byron-overview]

He separated from Anne Isabella Milbanke in 1816 and left England. Ada had no personal relationship with him. Byron died in 1824, when Ada was eight years old.[^ada-childhood]
