# Charles Wheatstone

Charles Wheatstone (1802–1875) was an English scientist and inventor.[^wheatstone-overview]

As Babbage's friend, he commissioned Ada to translate Menabrea's French article about the Analytical Engine into English.[^ada-notes]

His role in this bundle is “initiator of the translation.” Menabrea supplied the source text, while Ada's Notes emerged from her working relationship with Babbage.
