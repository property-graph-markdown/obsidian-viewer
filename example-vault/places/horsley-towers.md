# Horsley Towers

Horsley Towers is a nineteenth-century country house in East Horsley, Surrey. Charles Barry designed the original house, and William King-Noel later expanded it to his own designs.[^horsley-towers-overview]

The house is located in [Surrey](surrey.md "{type: located_in}").

From 1845, it was the principal home of [Ada Lovelace](../people/ada-lovelace.md "{type: \"residence_of\", from: 1845}") and her family.[^ada-adult-years]

The change is represented by [1845 — Horsley Towers Becomes the Family's Main Home](../events/1845-horsley-towers-main-home.md).
