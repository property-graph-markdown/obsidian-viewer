# London

London in England was Ada Lovelace's birthplace, a recurring residence, and the place of her death.[^ada-overview]

On 5 June 1833, Ada met Charles Babbage through Mary Somerville at one of his London soirées. Later that month, she saw the prototype of his Difference Engine.[^babbage-soirees] [^ada-overview]

After Ada's marriage, the family's residences continued to include a house in London.[^ada-adult-years]
