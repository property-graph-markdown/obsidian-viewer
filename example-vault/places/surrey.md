# Surrey

Surrey is a county in England containing Ockham Park and Horsley Towers, two residences associated with Ada's married life.[^ada-adult-years]

After the marriage in 1835, Ockham Park was one of the couple's three homes. Horsley Towers became the family's principal house in 1845.[^ada-adult-years]

The bundle describes Surrey as a residential and possible working environment, but does not claim a particular desk or institutional workplace without evidence.
