# Turin

Turin in Italy was the site of Charles Babbage's 1840 lecture about the Analytical Engine.[^ada-notes]

Luigi Menabrea turned that lecture into a French text published in 1842. Ada Lovelace translated and annotated the text in 1842–1843.[^engine-overview]

Turin therefore belongs to the origin chain of Ada's principal publication. It is explicitly not modeled as a documented travel destination or workplace of Ada herself.
