The 46 Concept documents, their PGM structure, and original project text were
prepared for the Property Graph Markdown project and are Copyright (c) 2026
Property Graph Markdown contributors.

Historical facts and adapted wording are attributed in each Concept document
to the linked English Wikipedia page or section, whose contributors publish
under compatible Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike terms.

The documents summarize, reorganize, and annotate those sources as an OKF 0.2
Knowledge Bundle with PGM 0.4.0 Node and Relationship properties. Wikipedia
page histories provide the contributor attribution for each cited page; open
the cited page and select its History view to inspect those contributors.

The specification, reference parser, tests, and other software in this
repository remain available under the repository's MIT License.
