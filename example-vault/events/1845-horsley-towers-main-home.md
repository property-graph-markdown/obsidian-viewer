# 1845 — Horsley Towers Becomes the Family's Main Home

Horsley Towers in Surrey became the Lovelace family's principal house in 1845.[^ada-adult-years]

The residence was [Horsley Towers](../places/horsley-towers.md "{type: occurred_at, from: 1845}"), the principal home of Ada Lovelace, William King-Noel, and their children from 1845.
