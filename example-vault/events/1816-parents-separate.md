# 1816 — Ada's Parents Separate

Lady Byron left the shared household with five-week-old Ada on 16 January 1816; Lord Byron signed the deed of separation on 21 April.[^ada-childhood]

It involved Ada Lovelace, Anne Isabella Milbanke, and Lord Byron.
