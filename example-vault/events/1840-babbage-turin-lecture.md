# 1840 — Babbage Lectures in Turin

Charles Babbage gave a seminar about the Analytical Engine at the University of Turin in 1840, and Luigi Menabrea recorded the lecture in French.[^ada-notes]

It involved Charles Babbage and Luigi Menabrea, but not Ada directly.

It took place in [Turin](../places/turin.md "{type: occurred_at}") and concerned the [Analytical Engine](../work/analytical-engine.md "{type: concerns}").
