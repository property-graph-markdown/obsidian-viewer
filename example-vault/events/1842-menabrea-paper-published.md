# 1842 — Menabrea's Analytical Engine Paper Is Published

Luigi Menabrea's French account of Babbage's Analytical Engine lecture was published in October 1842 in the *Bibliothèque universelle de Genève*.[^ada-notes]

The author was Luigi Menabrea, and the subject was the [Analytical Engine](../work/analytical-engine.md "{type: concerns}").
