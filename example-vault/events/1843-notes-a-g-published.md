# 1843 — Ada's Translation and Notes A–G Are Published

Ada's English translation and seven Notes were published under her initials AAL in *Taylor's Scientific Memoirs* in 1843.[^ada-notes]

The publication connected Ada Lovelace, Luigi Menabrea, and Charles Babbage.

The published work is represented by [Menabrea Translation and Notes A–G](../work/menabrea-translation-and-notes.md "{type: resulted_in}").
