# 1842–1843 — Ada Translates Menabrea's Paper

Over nine months in 1842–1843, Ada translated Menabrea's French paper into English and expanded it with seven Notes, A through G.[^ada-notes]

It involved Ada Lovelace, Luigi Menabrea, Charles Babbage, and Charles Wheatstone in distinct roles.

The result was the [Menabrea translation and Notes A–G](../work/menabrea-translation-and-notes.md "{type: resulted_in}").
