# 1836 — Byron King-Noel Is Born

Ada and William's eldest child, Byron King-Noel, was born in 1836.[^ada-adult-years]

The child was [Byron King-Noel, Viscount Ockham](../people/byron-king-noel.md "{type: birth_of}"); his parents were Ada Lovelace and William King-Noel.
