# 1851 — Ada Mentions Work Connecting Mathematics and Music

In 1851, Ada wrote to her mother about “certain productions” she was developing on the relationship between mathematics and music.[^ada-work]

It involved Ada Lovelace as the researcher and Anne Isabella Milbanke as the recipient of the letter.
