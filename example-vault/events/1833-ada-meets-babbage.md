# 1833 — Ada Meets Charles Babbage

Mary Somerville introduced Ada to Charles Babbage at one of his London soirées on 5 June 1833. The meeting led to a long friendship and collaboration.[^babbage-soirees]

It involved Ada Lovelace, Charles Babbage, and Mary Somerville.

The meeting took place in [London](../places/london.md "{type: occurred_at}").
