# 1953 — Ada's Notes Are Republished

Ada's Notes on the Analytical Engine were republished in 1953 as an appendix to B. V. Bowden's *Faster than Thought*.[^ada-program]

The republication renewed attention to [Ada Lovelace](../people/ada-lovelace.md "{type: commemorates, role: \"subject\"}") and the [Menabrea translation and Notes A–G](../work/menabrea-translation-and-notes.md "{type: concerns}").
