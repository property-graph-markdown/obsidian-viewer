# 1839 — Ralph Gordon Is Born

Ada and William's youngest child, Ralph Gordon, was born in 1839.[^ada-adult-years]

The child was [Ralph King-Milbanke, 2nd Earl of Lovelace](../people/ralph-king-milbanke.md "{type: birth_of}"); his parents were Ada Lovelace and William King-Noel.
