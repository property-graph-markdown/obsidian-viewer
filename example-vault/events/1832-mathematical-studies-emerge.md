# 1832 — Ada's Mathematical Studies Intensify

From 1832, when Ada was seventeen, her mathematical abilities became increasingly evident and mathematics became a central adult interest.[^ada-education]

The learner was Ada Lovelace, whose private teachers included Mary Somerville.
