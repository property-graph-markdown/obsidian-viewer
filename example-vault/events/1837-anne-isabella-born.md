# 1837 — Anne Isabella Is Born

Ada and William's daughter Anne Isabella, called Annabella, was born in 1837.[^ada-adult-years]

The child was [Anne Blunt, 15th Baroness Wentworth](../people/anne-blunt.md "{type: birth_of}"); her parents were Ada Lovelace and William King-Noel.
